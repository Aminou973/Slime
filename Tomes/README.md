## Where is anime Season 4, Cour 2 (Jul–Sep 2026)?

The anime adapts the **light novel**, which rewrites and greatly expands the WN, so the match is approximate.
Season 4 adapts roughly LN volumes 10–12. In this WN, Cour 2 matches:

- **End of the Demon Lord's Gambit Arc, Ch 130–135 (p. 4094–4282).** Leon's invitation, Rimuru meets Leon, Leon fights Yuuki, and Yuuki escapes.
- **First half of The Empire Invades! Arc, Ch 137–150 (p. 4284–4773).** These chapters cover:
  - the year of progress and the Council's request (Ch 137–141)
  - Tempest's military reorganization (Ch 142)
  - Diablo recruiting the primordial demons Testarossa, Ultima and Carrera (Ch 143, "The Dreadful Corps")
  - the Empire's scouts in the Underground Labyrinth, including Kondou (Ch 144–146)
  - the Empire's moves and the night before the war (Ch 147–150)

The Great War itself (Ch 151 onward) comes after this.
