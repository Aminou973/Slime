## Where is anime Season 4, Cour 2 (Jul–Sep 2026)?

The anime adapts the **light novel** (LN), which rewrites and reorders the web novel (WN).
Cour 2 starts at episode 13, "New Companions," which is the title of LN Volume 11, Chapter 2.
The episodes before it match the end of Volume 10 and Chapter 1 of Volume 11:

- Ep 9 "Investigating the Ruins": the Amalita ruins
- Ep 10 "The Master of Greed": Mariabell
- Ep 11 "Milim's Friend" and Ep 12 "Tempest Evolves": Volume 11, Chapter 1

So **Cour 2 ≈ LN Volume 11, Chapter 2 to the end, plus the start of Volume 12.**
LN chapter titles come from the fan translation at https://tensurafan.github.io/.

| LN (Cour 2) | What happens | Web novel equivalent (this PDF) |
|---|---|---|
| Vol 11 Ch 2 – New Companions | Diablo recruits the primordials Testarossa, Ultima and Carrera | **Ch 143 – The Dreadful Corps** (p. 4469–4511) |
| Vol 11 Ch 3 – Signs of Unrest | Yuuki and Kagali make their move; Hinata and the children | **Ch 121–122** (p. 3821–3875) |
| Vol 11 Ch 4 – Western Turmoil | Hinata is attacked; Leon and Luminas get involved | **Ch 123** (p. 3876–3913) |

| Vol 12 Prologue & Ch 1 | Yuuki on the run; meeting with Guy, Leon and Luminas | **Ch 130–135** (p. 4094–4282), Leon vs Yuuki |

In short, in this PDF Cour 2 is mostly the **Demon Lord's Gambit Arc (file 07)**, Ch 121–135,
plus **Ch 143 from The Empire Invades! Arc (file 08)**, where the primordial demons join.
The LN storylines about Mariabell, the Council and the Amalita ruins (Cour 1) do not exist in the web novel.
